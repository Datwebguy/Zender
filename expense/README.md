# Zender task accounts

Give an agent a job, a budget and permission to buy the services it needs. Review larger purchases, follow payment and delivery, and keep a private expense record.

## The workflow

1. Create a task and choose its budget, services and expiry.
2. Connect your agent with a credential for that task.
3. Review purchases that need your approval.
4. Receive the result and download the expense record.

The budget includes network fees. Closing a task stops new purchases and revokes its agent access. A payment already sent remains in its history.
